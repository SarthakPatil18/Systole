Before the camera goes on

Systole listens to your pulse and breathing through your webcam and turns them into music. Here is exactly what that involves.

  • Your camera sees you; your device does the maths. Frames are read in this window by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK. Pulse, breathing and HRV are computed on your machine.
  • Nothing is recorded, saved, or uploaded by Systole. No video, no audio from the camera (the mic is never opened), no vitals history, no session log. Close the window and the session is gone.
  • One network call, and it has nothing to do with your body. The SDK authenticates your Presage API key and checks the subscription. That is the only traffic Systole causes.
  • Diagnostics are off unless you say otherwise. Presage's SDK can send aggregate session summaries (no video, no measured values). The switch below is off by default.
  • Stop whenever you like. Stop releases the camera immediately; macOS shows the camera indicator whenever it is live.

Kept in memory for this session only — never written to disk.

Systole is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any condition. Metrics are for general wellness and entertainment.
